Private events

Book the whole room

A cozy, inviting space for the nights that matter: book clubs, birthdays, showers, writing groups, team nights.

How it works

👥

Up to 30 guests

The room holds private reservations of up to 30 people, which is exactly the size where everyone still hears each other.

💳

A deposit holds your date

A 50% reservation deposit books the space. If plans shift, that value moves to a future reservation, so a rain check never costs you the money.

☕

Coffee included, obviously

Drink service from the bar, a coffee-to-go box option for the table, and pastries to go with it.

Ask about a date

Email gets the fastest answer. Reservation rates are quoted when you ask.
